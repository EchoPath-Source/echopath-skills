# OSF Pre-Registration Generator — Phase Outline

## Phase 1 — Intake and framing

Collect the study title, research question, field, theory/rationale, and whether data collection has started.

## Phase 2 — Hypotheses

Separate confirmatory hypotheses from exploratory ideas. Convert vague claims into falsifiable statements.

## Phase 3 — Quantitative predictions

Ask what direction, magnitude, threshold, or pattern would support or fail to support the hypothesis.

## Phase 4 — Variables and operational definitions

Define independent variables, dependent variables, covariates, controls, and measurement procedures.

## Phase 5 — Methods

Capture design type, recruitment or data source, procedure, materials, instruments, and collection timeline.

## Phase 6 — Analysis plan

Specify primary tests/models, secondary analyses, robustness checks, exclusion rules, missing data handling, and exploratory analyses.

## Phase 7 — Power and sample justification

Request planned sample size, power calculation if available, or practical justification when power calculation is not possible.

## Phase 8 — Bias reduction

Document blinding, randomization, controls, stopping rules, data quality checks, and ethical constraints.

## Phase 9 — Final assembly

Produce a polished OSF-ready draft, unresolved TBD list, and submission checklist.
