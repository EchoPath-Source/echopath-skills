# OSF Pre-Registration Generator

A lightweight AI-assisted workflow for drafting OSF-ready pre-registration plans before data collection.

## What it does

This skill guides a researcher through the core parts of a rigorous pre-registration:

- Study framing and research question
- Hypotheses and falsifiable predictions
- Variables and operational definitions
- Methods and participant/sample plan
- Analysis plan
- Power analysis notes
- Blinding, controls, exclusions, and stopping rules
- Timeline, versioning, and final assembly

## Who it is for

- Independent researchers
- Citizen scientists
- Students
- Small labs
- Writers preparing open-science research plans
- Anyone who wants to make a study harder to retroactively reshape after results are known
