## Description

The OSF Pre-Registration Generator Lite helps researchers structure a pre-registration draft with clearer hypotheses, quantitative predictions, variables, methods, analysis plans, bias controls, and final review checklists.

This public/lite version is designed for discovery and basic use. A paid expanded version can include additional templates, examples, and full marketplace assets.
